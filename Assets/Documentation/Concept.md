# Concept \- Final Project

**Name:** **Glenn Magnell**   
**Game title: CUEPOINT**  
**Type:** **3D**

One-page concept for a short first-person rhythm shooter. One selected music track per run, played in a futuristic corridor.

---

## Player Fantasy

The feeling the player is really there for, as one sentence: "I am a rhythm shooter moving through a futuristic corridor, hitting targets precisely on the beat while staying in flow with the music.." In a 5-minute game, it's one moment, not a whole adventure.

## Pillars

1\. The beat drives the action. Targets are timed to the music, so listening and timing matter more than shooting randomly.

2\. Movement stays simple and readable. The player moves forward automatically but is able to switch between three lanes while jumping and sliding.

3\. Every shot gives clear feedback. Hits, misses and timing accuracy are communicated through sound, animation and score feedback.  
> 

## Core Loop

Listen to the beat → move between lanes or jump/slide → aim and shoot targets in time→ receive timing feedback and score → continue through the track.

## Win and/or Lose

\- Win: Complete the selected music track and reach the end of the corridor. The final score is based on shooting and timing accuracy.

\- Lose: There is no separate fail state more than that you respawn because of missed jump or slide  
> 

## Risk

The targets, shooting windows and music may not stay synchronized. Even a small timing difference could make shots feel unfair or disconnected from the beat.

First test: Build a simple greybox section with one music track and a few targets at known times. Use the same timing reference for the music and target events, then test whether the targets appear at the intended beats and whether the shooting window feels fair.

## Opportunity

The combination of first-person movement, rhythm-based shooting and strong audio-visual feedback could give CUEPOINT a distinctive identity. 

Different music tracks can also give players a reason to replay and improve their timing and scores. 

Possible adds later on: Leaderboard, More maps, Creative Mode (Build your own map), Skin shop, 1V1 ranked mode.
